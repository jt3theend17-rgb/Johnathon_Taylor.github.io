RESEARCH

Higher categorical algebra and homotopy theory.

My research studies algebraic presentations of higher-dimensional structures and their interaction with homotopy theory.

01

Higher categorical algebra

I investigate algebraic structures in dimensions two and three, including coherence, higher composition, and categorical structures whose operations are organized by algebraic theories.

02

Infinity-groupoids

My work includes Grothendieck infinity-groupoids, orientals, nerves, Kan complexes, and algebraic approaches to the homotopy hypothesis.

03

Lawvere theories and monads

I study Lawvere theories, limit sketches, monads, and distributive series of monads as mechanisms for constructing higher algebraic theories inductively.

04

Controlled theories

Controlled theories augment algebraic presentations with strict control data that governs lifting problems and provides a bridge between algebraic and homotopical structure.

05

Model structures

I am interested in algebraic weak factorization systems, cofibrant generation, semi-model structures, and transferred model structures for higher categorical objects.

06

Higher coherence

Longer-term directions include double and triple infinity-groupoids, Gray-like tensor products, higher nerves, rewriting systems, and geometric models for coherence.
