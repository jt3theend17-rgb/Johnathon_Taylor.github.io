PERSONAL

A little more about me.

Mathematics is an important part of who I am, but it is not the only part. This is a place to share some of the people, places, and experiences that have shaped me.

HOMETOWN

Gary, Indiana.

Gary, Indiana is my hometown, and I love Gary. It is a place that has shaped who I am and how I see the world. Gary has a complicated history and is often portrayed only through its struggles, but my connection to the city is personal: it is home, and I am proud to come from there. Gary is also the hometown of Michael Jackson, one of my favorite musicians and my husband's favorite musician. His connection to Gary is another part of what makes the city special to me. I also graduated from Gary Lighthouse College Preparatory Academy, making my connection to Gary one that is deeply rooted in both my childhood and education.

PÉTANQUE

A game from Southern France.

Outside of mathematics, I play pétanque and am part of a local league. Pétanque is a French boules sport with roots in Southern France, where the modern game developed in the early twentieth century. I enjoy the combination of strategy, precision, and camaraderie that comes with playing, and being part of a league has given me a wonderful community beyond mathematics.
