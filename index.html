RESEARCH

Structure, coherence, and homotopy.

My research concerns mathematical structures in which composition and coherence become increasingly rich in higher dimensions.

01

Higher categorical algebra

Algebraic structures in dimensions two and three, higher categories, coherence, and the algebraic organization of higher-dimensional composition.

02

Infinity-groupoids

Grothendieck infinity-groupoids, nerves, orientals, Kan complexes, and models for spaces through higher categorical structure.

03

Algebraic theories

Lawvere theories, controlled theories, monads, sketches, and algebraic presentations of higher-dimensional mathematical structures.

04

Homotopy theory

Model structures, algebraic weak factorization systems, homotopification, and the relationship between algebraic and homotopical structure.

ABOUT

Mathematics as structure, coherence, and geometry.

I am a mathematician working in higher categorical algebra. My work explores how complicated mathematical structures can be organized by algebraic theories and how these structures interact with homotopy theory.

I am particularly interested in the passage between strict algebraic data and the higher coherence required to model genuinely higher-dimensional phenomena.
